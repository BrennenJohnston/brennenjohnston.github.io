The Plug Puller is an assistive tool for people with limited grip strength or hand dexterity. It straps around the body of an electrical plug, adding large finger holes and a solid handle so the plug can be pulled straight out of the outlet without bending fingernails, yanking the cord, or damaging the plug or outlet.

This is the original fixed-size design: print it, attach it to your plug, and it's ready to use. It's part of WATAP's free 3D-printed assistive technology catalog. Washington state residents can [request a printed copy for free](https://watap.org/3d-printed-assistive-technology), and anyone can download and print their own from the links above.

For an adjustable version you can size to your own plug and hand, see the [Customizable Plug Puller v1]({{ '/projects/plug-puller/' | relative_url }}).
