This is a work-in-progress, OpenSCAD-customizable version of the [Plug Puller](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5936465), an assistive tool for people with limited grip strength or hand dexterity. The two printed halves strap securely around the body of an electrical plug with zip ties, adding large finger holes and a solid handle so the plug can be pulled straight out of the outlet — without bending fingernails, yanking the cord, or damaging the plug or outlet.

The original fixed-size Plug Puller is part of WATAP's free 3D-printed assistive technology catalog. This OpenSCAD version aims to make the design adjustable so it can fit more plug shapes and hand sizes.
