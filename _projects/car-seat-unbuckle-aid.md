Child car seat buckles take a surprising amount of thumb force to release — a real barrier for parents and caregivers with arthritis, joint pain, or limited hand strength. This printed aid fits over the buckle and adds leverage so the release button can be pressed without straining a thumb.

The Child Car Seat Unbuckle Aid is part of WATAP's free 3D-printed assistive technology catalog — Washington State residents can [request a printed copy for free](https://watap.org/3d-printed-assistive-technology), and anyone can download and print their own.

## What's next: v2.0

A more ergonomic redesign is in progress. The v2.0 prototypes pictured above rework the handle into a rounded, keyhole-shaped grip and experiment with textured surfaces for a more secure hold with less hand effort. The design will be shared very soon — check back, or reach out through the [contact options]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) if you'd like to help test it.
