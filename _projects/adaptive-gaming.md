This is an ongoing WATAP initiative around adaptive gaming. We're expanding WATAP's support by assessing current gaming accessibility and creating custom solutions to bridge the gaps left by existing adaptive controllers — so more people can enjoy full gaming experiences.

Existing adaptive hardware (like the Xbox Adaptive Controller and Microsoft Adaptive Accessories) covers a lot of ground, but every player's needs are different. 3D printing lets us fill the remaining gaps with custom mounts, switch holders, thumbsticks, and other one-off adaptations.
