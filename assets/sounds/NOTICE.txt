The sounds of this site.

Each sound file here is cut from a recording another person made and
shared. The changes are the same for every file: a short piece cut out,
mixed to one channel, a short rise at its start and a fade at its end, its
level changed, and saved as WAV (48,000 Hz, 16 bit). The untouched
recordings, the pages they came from and their checksums are in
vendor/pristine/sounds/, and vendor/pristine/PROVENANCE.md lists them.
